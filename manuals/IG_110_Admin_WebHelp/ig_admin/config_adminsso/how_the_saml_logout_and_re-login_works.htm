How the SAML logout (and re-login) works

This section describes the SAML logout process:

1.      The administrator, Bob, clicks the Logout link in the Administration interface.

2.      Bob is logged out of the Administration interface. He remains logged in at any other SAML SP sites to which he is currently logged in.

3.      Bob attempts to log in to the Administration interface again, and is redirected to the Federation Module to log in with the configured authentication methods.

Note: The SAML single logout feature—where logging out of one affiliated SP logs you out of all of them—is not supported.
