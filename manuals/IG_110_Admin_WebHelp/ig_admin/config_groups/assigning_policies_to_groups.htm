IdentityGuard Server 11.0 Administration Guide

Assigning policies to groups

Create your initial policies, if needed, before you create your initial groups. Policies are associated with groups. See Initial administration workflow.

An administrator can associate a policy with a group if:

●       the administrator has the groupSet permission in a role

●       the group is in the group list of at least one of the administrator’s roles, or the role applies to all groups (the role must have the groupSet permission)

●       the policy is in the policy list of at least one of the administrator’s roles, or the role applies to all policies (the role must have the groupSet permission)

You can associate a policy with a group during either group creation or subsequent modification. For details, see Create a group or Modify a group.
