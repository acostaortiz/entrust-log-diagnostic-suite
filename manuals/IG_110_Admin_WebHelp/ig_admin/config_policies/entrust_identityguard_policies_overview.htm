IdentityGuard Server 11.0 Administration Guide

Entrust IdentityGuard policies overview

Policies determine how features are defined by your organization and applied to Entrust IdentityGuard operations.

Entrust IdentityGuard has several categories of policies available from the Policy Category list of the Policies pages.

Policies are assigned to groups and affect all users in that group. Cards and tokens are also assigned to groups, and card and token policies are determined by their associated group.

Entrust IdentityGuard provides the ability for administrators, with the applicable permissions, to create, change, and delete policies. The policies that administrators can access are limited to those in the policy lists for their roles.
