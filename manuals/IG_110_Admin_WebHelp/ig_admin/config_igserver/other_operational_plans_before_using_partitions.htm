IdentityGuard Server 11.0 Administration Guide

Other operational plans before using partitions

Setting up partitions is an advanced use of Entrust IdentityGuard and requires careful planning. Because you would normally use partitions only in an organization with multiple sites and multiple data repositories that must be kept in synchronization with each other, there are many operational issues that must be arranged before partitions are implemented.

Operational plans must be put in place in your organization to ensure that your replication schedule and procedures safeguard your operation from duplication and from overwriting needed data.
