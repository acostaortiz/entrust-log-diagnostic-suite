IdentityGuard Server 11.0 Administration Guide

Step 16: Give Self-Service more permissions

You must ensure that the Self-Service Module’s account in Entrust IdentityGuard has the correct permissions. The superuser role in Entrust IdentityGuard has the appropriate permissions, so if you are certain that this role is assigned to the Self-Service account, then you can skip this procedure.

Otherwise, follow the procedure to ensure Self-Service has the correct permissions.

To ensure correct permissions

1.      Determine which account the Self-Service Module is using:

a.      Log in to the Self-Service Module’s Configuration interface. For details, see the Entrust IdentityGuard Self-Service Installation and Configuration Guide.

b.      Click Properties.

c.      From the table of contents, click Entrust IdentityGuard Administration Service Settings (first item).

d.      Take note of the Administrator User ID. In the image below, it is ssmadmin. This is the name of the account that Self-Service uses to access Entrust IdentityGuard server.

You must check that this account has the appropriate permissions, as described in the next steps.

2.      Log in to the Entrust IdentityGuard Administration interface.

3.      From the top menu, click User Accounts and search for the user account (ssmadmin in this example).

4.      Determine the role assigned to the user. In the image, it is igssmrole.

5.      Do one of the following:

●       If the role is superuser, then you can skip this procedure and go to the next section. superuser has the correct permissions.

●       If the role is not superuser (for example, igssmrole), follow the remaining steps in this procedure to assign permissions.

6.      From the menu at the top, click Roles.

A list of roles appears.

7.      Click igssmrole, or whatever role is assigned to the Self-Service Module account.

8.      Under Select a permission category, select Managed CA.

Ensure that you meet these minimum permissions:

9.      Click Done.

You have now made sure that the Self-Service’s account has the appropriate permissions.
