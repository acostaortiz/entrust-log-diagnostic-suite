View the serial number of an Entrust Mini Token

The serial number is useful for identifying the Entrust Mini Token in case the token’s back sticker comes off.

You must give the serial number to a Help Desk operator to resynchronize the token.

To view the serial number

1.      Press and hold down the button on the Entrust Mini Token until the status bar fills the display.

The token generates and displays the dynamic password.

2.      While the password is still displayed, press and hold down the button again until the text V Sn displays.

The serial number appears as scrolling text in two parts of 5 digits each, preceded by the part number:

●       1 XXXXX

●       2 XXXXX

Where X represents a number.

Note: The help desk will combine the two lines to create the serial number.

3.      To turn off the token, press the button again.

The token turns off automatically after 20 seconds of inactivity.
