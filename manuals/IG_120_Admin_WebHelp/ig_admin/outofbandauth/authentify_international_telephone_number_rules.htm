Authentify international telephone number rules

To be recognized by the Authentify service, the telephone numbers entered in Entrust IdentityGuard user contact information must agree with the rules in the following table.

Portion of telephone number

Number of digits

Rules

Required/optional

Country code

1-3 digits

Must be preceded by + and followed by a space or hyphen

Required for non-North American locations

North America domestic long distance code

1 digit; preceded by “+”

This is always “+1”

Optional

Area or city code

2 or 3 digits

Required

Local telephone number

7 digits for locations in North America

4 to 13 digits for the  rest of the world

Required

Office extension

1 to 6 digits; preceded by an “x”

Optional

Examples of valid non-North America telephone numbers (these examples are from Australia and France):

●       +61 29 231 5555

●       +61 02 9231 5555

●       +33 01 56 78 90 12

●       +33 (01) 56 78 90 12 x200

●       +33-01-56-78-90-12x200

Examples of valid North America telephone numbers:

●       +1(613) 555 1234

●       613-555-5678 x3429

●       (613)555-5678x3429

Note: To interpret error messages returned by the Authentify service, refer to the Authentify documentation.
