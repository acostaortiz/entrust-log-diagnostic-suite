–  Configure delivery of OTPs in voice messages

–  Configure delivery of OTPs in SMS messages

●       Administering out-of-band one-time passwords

●       Downloading and installing third-party SSL certificates

Note: The term “password” appears often in Entrust IdentityGuard documentation. For clarification, an out-of-band one-time password, Entrust IdentityGuard password, token password (also known as a token one-time-password), personal verification number, and temporary PIN all refer to separate authentication methods.
