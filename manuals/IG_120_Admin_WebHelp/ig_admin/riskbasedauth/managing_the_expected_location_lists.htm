Managing the expected location lists

In managing the expected location lists, first edit the policy’s expected location list. Include just locations that a majority of users in the group associated with that policy are likely to connect from.

Note: The administrator must have the appropriate permissions (userGetExpectedLocations and userSetExpectedLocations).

For an enterprise application, this could be just internal private IP addresses, or cities near the office where the people in the group work. For users who travel or work from remote locations, the administrator can add entries to their personal expected location list. For a consumer application, the list should include locations representing a majority of online customers.

When administrators add entries to a list, Entrust IdentityGuard ensures that the country and region codes are valid, but does not verify the city entry or the ISP entry.

To set policy related to expected locations, see Configuring RBA policies.
