Editing VPN challenge strings

The challenge messages are completely in your control. You can change the text in the default strings, remove placeholders (such as those for serial numbers), or you can add placeholders. You can use a parameter more than once; just make sure the edited fragments make sense when strung together in the complete message.

Note: The Radius protocol limits the each Radius message to a maximum of 253 characters. Create challenge messages so that the combination of the retry message and the challenge message have fewer than 253 characters.
If the Radius messages are too long, an error is logged in the igradius_system.log.

For example, assume you want to discourage the use of a temporary PIN, and encourage the use of tokens. In this case, change the text value for 1 in parameter 4 of the token message to something like this:

{4, choice, 0# a response from your Entrust IdentityGuard Token| 1# your temporary PIN. Register for your token today}

For users with no PVN or token, this is what they see:

Enter your temporary PIN. Register for your token today.

For users with a PVN but no token, this is what they see:

Enter your PVN and your temporary PIN. Register for your token today.

In the message syntax examples list under VPN challenge string reference, line breaks were added to enhance readability. Do not include line breaks if you change the challenge string properties. For example, the actual token message looks like this:

Enter{3, choice, 0#| 1# your PVN and}{4, choice, 0# a response from your token| 1# your temporary PIN}{5, choice, 0#|1# with serial number {0} or {1} or your temporary PIN|2# with serial number {0} or {1}|3# with serial number {0} or your temporary PIN|4# with serial number {0}|5# with a serial number from the list {8} or your temporary PIN|6# with a serial number from the list {8}}{7, choice, 0#|1# using the token challenge {2}}{6, choice, 0#.|1# and update your PVN twice in succession}{9, choice, 0#|1# (PVN size: {9}).}
