IdentityGuard Server 12.0 Administration Guide

External Risk Score policies

These policies support the use of external risk engines as part of adaptive, or risk-based, authentication..

 

Normal Passing Risk Score

Default: 0

Normal Failing Risk Score

Default: 100

Enhanced Passing Risk Score

Default: 0

Enhanced Failing Risk Score

Default: 100

Risk Engine

Iovation

 

The risk scoring parameters for the Normal and Enhanced security levels must be configured according to the requirements of the specific risk engine selected. The risk scoring begins at 0 (which indicates the lowest possible risk) and increases (which indicates a higher possible risk).

Entrust Datacard TransactionGuard™

You must consult with the group responsible for configuring your TransactionGuard service to determine what values are appropriate for the two security levels.

iovation SureScore™

The iovation SureScore integrated risk engine leverages the summary risk decision returned by the SureScore service. For this reason, the only requirement for the configuration of the risk scoring parameters, for the two security levels, is that the passing and failing scores differ by at least 10. Entrust Datacard recommends the following values:

●       Normal Passing Risk Score: 25

●       Normal Failing Risk Score: 100

●       Enhanced Passing Risk Score: 0

●       Enhanced Failing Risk Score: 50

Custom integrations

Consult the group responsible for deploying and configuring your custom External Risk Engine integration to determine the values that are appropriate for the normal and enhanced security levels.
