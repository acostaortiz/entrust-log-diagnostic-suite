IdentityGuard Server 13.0 Administration Guide

Authenticate with your Entrust Datacard OT token

Complete this procedure when presented with an authentication challenge (typically on a website or application) that asks you to enter a one-time password from your token.

To authenticate with the Entrust Datacard OT token

1.      Press the button to turn on the token.

The token displays a one-time password (a numeric code).

2.      Enter the password in response to the authentication challenge.

The token will turn off automatically after 30 seconds, or you can press the button again to turn off the display and conserve battery power.
