How adaptive authentication with iovation SureScore works

A user can have many devices through which they connect to resources protected by Entrust IdentityGuard. With the iovation SureScore external risk engine integration configured, Entrust IdentityGuard can reduce disruptive authentication challenges when devices are recognized and used legitimately, while safeguarding against fraudulent access.

The following sequence of events describes a typical use of this feature.

1.      By entering her user ID and PIN, Jane logs in to her banking website (protected by Entrust IdentityGuard) for the first time from her new mobile phone. There is no record of any actions involving that phone in the iovation SureScore database, so the phone is not trusted as a device that can access Jane's bank account. The Web site displays a second-factor authentication challenge, for example, a soft token challenge.

2.      Jane's identity is confirmed when she completes the soft token challenge successfully using her soft token app and she is granted access to her bank account.

3.      Following this successful authentication, Entrust IdentityGuard creates a pairing between Jane's Entrust IdentityGuard account and her mobile phone.

4.      The next day Jane logs in to her banking Web site again using her mobile phone. This time, after she enters her  user ID and PIN, the website recognizes the attributes of Jane's phone, establishing it as the same one she used to complete a second-factor authentication challenge previously. In addition, the external database confirms that the device has not been involved in fraud or misconduct in any other online events up to this point. As a result, Jane is granted access to her online banking account without having to complete a second-factor challenge.

Subsequent actions can affect whether the paired device continues to be trusted by Entrust IdentityGuard. For example,

●       If Jane enters her PIN incorrectly too many times when she attempts to log in to her bank account on another occasion and is locked out of her account, she could be presented with a second-factor challenge the next time she logs in after recovering her PIN.

●       If Jane's mobile phone is stolen by someone and used to perform fraudulent transactions online, the reputation of the phone is damaged. The iovation SureScore integration returns a recommendation to challenge or deny attempts to authenticate as Jane to websites or applications protected by Entrust IdentityGuard.
