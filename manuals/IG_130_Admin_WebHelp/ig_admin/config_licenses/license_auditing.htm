IdentityGuard Server 13.0 Administration Guide

Configure license auditing

Entrust IdentityGuard services perform a periodic license audit.

License auditing helps you determine when to renew your licenses or when you are about to run out of licenses. When you reach your license limits, contact Entrust for more licenses.

The administrator needs the correct permissions to manage licenses. For more information, see System and license permissions.
