Forcing token activation

When new Entrust IdentityGuard users are first assigned grid cards or tokens, they can continue to log in to your system through their VPN or other connection using just their current first-factor authentication method—typically a user name and password—as long as Entrust IdentityGuard Radius or your custom application is set up to allow it. This gives users a grace period from when grid cards or tokens are assigned to when they are received.

A grid card or token becomes activated the first time the user responds to the applicable Entrust IdentityGuard challenge.

If the grace period is open-ended, it does not force users to move to grid or token authentication. That is, even after users receive the grid or token, they can ignore a grid or token challenge and continue to log in without using the grid card or token. Without safeguards, this represents a way to avoid this level of authentication.

Entrust IdentityGuard lets you provide a safeguard, through a policy setting, that requires new users to activate their new grid cards or tokens within a certain period. After the date implied by this grace period, the users’ access is blocked until they authenticate with the grid card or token. The choice of a grid card or token is set by policy.

After users authenticate with a grid card or token, they can never log in without the grid card or token authentication again, even if their grid card or token is canceled or on hold.
