IdentityGuard Server 13.0 Administration Guide

Sign a transaction with your Entrust Datacard CR token

Complete this procedure when presented with an online transaction that you are asked to digitally sign.

To sign a transaction with the Entrust Datacard CR token

1.      Press the power button to turn on the token.

2.      Enter your PIN, and then press the OK button.

3.      Press the down arrow button until Signature is highlighted in the display window, and then press the OK button.

4.      As directed by the Web page or application, enter one or more values associated with the transaction you are performing. For example, if you are transferring $5000 from one bank account to another, your banking website might ask you to enter your account numbers and the amount to be transferred.

These numbers are entered as one long string, for example, 1002319876545000, where 100231 and 987654 are your account numbers and 5000 is the amount.

5.      After entering each requested value, press OK. The token uses the information you entered, along with the time and your unique key, to generate a digital signature to enter in the requesting application.

6.      Follow any further instructions on the Web page or application.

7.      Press the power button to turn off the display and conserve battery power. When the OTP expires (30 seconds), the token displays the menu and then turns off automatically after about 30 seconds of inactivity.
