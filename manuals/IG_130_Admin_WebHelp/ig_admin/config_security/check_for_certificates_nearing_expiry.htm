IdentityGuard Server 13.0 Administration Guide

Check for certificates nearing expiry

Certificates must be valid for your Entrust IdentityGuard installation to function. The Key Store Management tab helps remind you when certificates are nearing expiry so you can begin the process of replacing them and avoid loss of service due to expired certificates.

Automatic notification

If any certificate in your key store will expire in less than 30 days or has expired, you see a notice on the Home page when you log in to a new session with the Administration interface.

To check for certificates nearing expiry

1.      Log in to the Administration interface.

2.      From the top menu, select System, then click the Key Store Management tab.

3.      View the list of key store contents.

●       If a private key or certificate is due to expire in less than 90 days, its row in the table is highlighted with yellow.

●       If a private key or certificate is due to expire within one week or has already expired, its row in the table is highlighted with red.

4.      If you hold your cursor over a red or yellow entry, a tool tip appears providing more information.

●       A red entry shows one of the following tool tips: "The certificate has already expired" or "This certificate will expire in less than one week."

●       A yellow entry shows this tool tip: "This certificate will expire in less than 90 days".
