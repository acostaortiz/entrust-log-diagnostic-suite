IdentityGuard Server 13.0 Administration Guide

Biometric enrollment overview

Currently, the only supported scenario for biometric authentication is the use of fingerprint biometrics for login to Windows computers using Entrust IdentityGuard Desktop for Microsoft Windows.

Enrollment for biometric authentication works as follows:

1.      An administrator gives Alice, a user, a fingerprint scanner to plug in to a USB port on her Windows computer along with a software installation package to set up the Entrust IdentityGuard Desktop client application. The administrator tells her to use this installer to install both the desktop client and the fingerprint enrollment client, so she can enroll fingerprints for biometric authentication.

2.      Alice runs the installer, specifying that both the desktop client and the fingerprint enrollment client should be installed.

3.      During the installation, the installer launches Alice’s default browser and opens her company’s self-service website. The installer provides a brief description of what she should do on the website.

Attention: As noted on the installer screen, users may complete the installer first and then enroll their fingerprints. However, it is important that they enroll their fingerprints before they lock their desktop (or before it locks automatically after a period of inactivity). If the desktop locks before the user’s fingerprints are enrolled, the user may be unable to log in to the Windows desktop.

4.      Alice logs in to the self-service website and then clicks the self-administration action I’d like to enroll for fingerprint biometric authentication.

5.      On the new web page that appears, Alice clicks Capture Fingerprint Data.

6.      On Alice’s desktop, the fingerprint enrollment client starts. It shows a pair of hands with ovals around the tips of the index fingers. These are the recommended fingers to enroll, but users can select other fingers to enroll or they can enroll fingers specified by the administrator.

7.      Following the on-screen instructions, Alice clicks a finger on the screen image, and then places the fingertip of her corresponding finger on the fingerprint scanner. After a few seconds, a green border appears around the fingerprint image and a progress bar shows that the fingerprint is partially enrolled.

8.      Alice removes her finger from the scanner and places the same finger on the scanner two more times, until 3 images have been captured. When three images have been scanned, a check mark appears on the image of the enrolled finger

9.      Alice repeats the enrollment steps for as two more fingers, and then clicks Done.

10.  The fingerprint enrollment client closes. Alice clicks Next on the installer and completes the installation.

11.  Alice can now log in to her computer using her enrolled fingerprints, as described in Overview of authenticating with fingerprint biometrics.

or

If the Entrust IdentityGuard Biometric policy is configured to require administrator approval of enrolled fingerprints, the administrator will let Alice know when she can begin using her enrolled fingerprints for biometric authentication on her Windows computer.
