Analyzing client application and administrator roles

Consider the following questions when determining which roles to assign to a client application or administrator:

●       What tasks must the client application or administrator perform?

●       What permissions are needed to perform those tasks?

●       What policies must be in the role policy list in the role to perform those tasks?

●       What groups must in the role group list to cover all the target users?

●       What roles must be in the role’s role list to cover all the target users (that is, for administrators)?

For each client application or administrator, you can then determine which permissions are required, what policies they need to access, and what groups the applicable users are in.
